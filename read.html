Your ADHD-friendly text will appear here.

What is this? The technique is called Bionic Reading: the first part of each word is emphasized so your eyes land on those anchors and your brain fills in the rest. Many people — especially with ADHD or dyslexia — find it easier to stay in the line. (The formal reading-speed studies are actually mixed, so treat it as a comfort tool, not a magic bullet — and tune the sliders until it feels right for you.)

Everything runs locally in your browser. Files and pasted text are never uploaded. Website links are fetched through a public text-extraction service, so some pages may not load — paste the text directly if one fails.
